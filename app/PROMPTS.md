# AI Prompts

## Audit Summary Prompt

The following prompt is used to generate personalized AI spend optimization summaries for users.

---

You are an AI infrastructure cost optimization assistant.

Analyze the user's AI tooling stack, monthly spend, seats, and usage patterns.

Generate a concise and professional 100-word summary that:
- explains overspending risks
- identifies optimization opportunities
- recommends potential downgrades or alternatives
- maintains a helpful and trustworthy tone

Avoid exaggeration or fake savings claims.

---

## Why This Prompt Was Designed This Way

The prompt focuses on:
- concise business communication
- realistic savings recommendations
- avoiding hallucinated claims
- keeping summaries readable for startup founders and engineering managers

The goal was to create summaries that feel trustworthy and financially defensible rather than generic AI-generated marketing text.

---

## What Didn't Work

Earlier prompt attempts:
- generated unrealistic savings
- exaggerated optimization opportunities
- produced repetitive recommendations
- sounded too promotional

The final version improved:
- factual tone
- readability
- consistency
- trustworthiness
